Why check in?

A quick check-in helps LifeOS understand how your life is really going. Just tell us how you’re feeling whenever you have a moment. You don’t need to write anything every day. LifeOS looks for patterns over time and helps you understand what’s changing and what may be affecting your happiness.

  1. You check in Whenever you have a moment
  2. LifeOS finds patterns Across your check-ins, goals, habits, and life areas
  3. You see what matters So you can focus and take action
